    Six Axes, One Record
    
        
    Every paper, read the same way.
    
        
    Instead of free-text tags, each record is annotated against six complementary questions — so you can cross-reference technique against outcome, or surface against measured risk, across the whole catalog.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    01 · WHEN
    Lifecycle
    Training, inference, or system — the stage at which a threat is introduced or a failure is evaluated.
    
        
    02 · WHERE
    Surface
    The interface directly manipulated or evaluated — perception, language, proprioception, planning, or control.
    
        
    03 · HOW
    Mechanism
    The concrete attack technique or failure mode studied — not a guess at the deepest internal cause.
    
        
    04 · TARGET
    Target
    What the study explicitly attacks, protects, or evaluates — perception model, policy, planner, or private data.
    
        
    05 · CONSEQUENCE
    Consequence
    The specific endpoint reached — from task failure and collision to control hijacking or privacy leakage.
    
        
    06 · RISK
    Measured risk
    The broad risk domain actually evaluated with a reported metric — reliability, physical safety, ethics, or security.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Six-axis taxonomy
    Classify research by lifecycle stage, surface, technique, target, consequence, and measured risk — defined with explicit annotation rules, not a loose tag cloud.
    
      
    Interactive cross-maps
    Click any cell to pivot straight into the papers behind it — surfacing relationships and coverage gaps a flat list can't show.
    
      
    LLM-assisted curation
    New papers are discovered and annotated daily, then checked against primary sources — a one-time full requalification pass runs after every taxonomy revision.
