Interactive views of the Awesome Physical AI Safety catalog ( papers).

Charts summarize catalog coverage, not field-wide prevalence. Click a cell to inspect the contributing papers.

Bubble size is the number of cataloged papers in a field-year cell. Hover for a preview; click a bubble to pin the paper list.

Cells show mechanism-surface co-occurrence for concrete threat models. Hover for a preview; click a cell to pin the paper list.

Rows are threat mechanisms and columns are empirically evaluated risk outcomes. Empty cells remain visible.
